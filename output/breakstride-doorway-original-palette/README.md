# Breakstride — Open Doorway, original palette

The Open Doorway geometry is unchanged. Colors were sampled from the existing iOS app icon:

- Violet background: #6A14DE
- Indigo background: #3726D0
- Blue background: #1A6BF5
- Pink-lavender symbol: #F6CAFB
- Ice-blue symbol: #9AC9FB

The gradients are a simplified vector interpretation of the original icon's palette, not a recreation of its lighting or texture.

logo-icon.svg and logo-icon.png are the rounded-square preview, with transparent corners. app-icon.svg and app-icon.png are full square artwork; the 1024 × 1024 app PNG is opaque. PNGs are rendered from the accompanying SVGs.

This is the approved Breakstride icon design. It has not yet been installed in the app or submitted to Apple.
